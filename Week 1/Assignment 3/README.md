# 🎯 Week 1 · Assignment 3 — What Are You Proving?
 
> AI Fluency Course
 
---
 
## 🎯 Brief
 
Write a one-paragraph proof statement answering three questions plainly: the one claim (one primary skill, named), the one person (a specific audience who could hire or engage you), and the one action (the single most important thing you want them to do). Then write one honest line on why this needs to exist — what a CV or LinkedIn alone can't prove.
 
---
## 📝 Proof Statement
 
> My portfolio proves to hiring managers looking for junior backend developers that I can build reliable, production-reasonable backend applications — shown through my projects, internship experience, and how I document my process from problem to solution. My goal is for them to reach out and offer me an internship opportunity.
 
---
 
## 💡 Why This Needs to Exist
 
> My CV and LinkedIn list my education and experience, but they can't show how I actually approach problems, build projects, or improve technically over time — only my portfolio can prove that.

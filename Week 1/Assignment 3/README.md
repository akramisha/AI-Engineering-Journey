## 🎯 Brief
 
Write a one-paragraph proof statement answering three questions plainly: the one claim (one primary skill, named), the one person (a specific audience who could hire or engage you), and the one action (the single most important thing you want them to do). Then write one honest line on why this needs to exist — what a CV or LinkedIn alone can't prove.

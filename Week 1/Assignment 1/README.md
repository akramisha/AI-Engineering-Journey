# Week 1 — Assignment 1
## AI Workflow Audit & Tool Setup

### Objective

The goal of this assignment was to analyse my weekly workflow and understand how Artificial Intelligence can support different types of tasks.

Rather than assuming AI should be used everywhere, the focus was on identifying where:

- Human judgement is essential
- AI can assist with review
- AI works best as a collaborator
- Certain repetitive tasks can be automated

---

## What I Completed

- Analysed my recurring weekly tasks
- Categorised each task based on AI involvement
- Explained the reasoning behind each decision
- Defined what successful completion looks like for every task
- Created a personalised Claude Project for future learning

---

## Key Takeaways

- AI is most effective when used intentionally rather than automatically.
- Critical thinking remains essential, especially for engineering tasks.
- Understanding **when** to use AI is just as important as knowing **how** to use it.
- Well-defined workflows make collaboration with AI much more effective.

---

## Skills Practiced

- Workflow Analysis
- AI Collaboration
- Critical Thinking
- Productivity Planning
- Prompt Awareness

---

## Reflection

This assignment helped me realise that AI is not a replacement for learning or problem-solving. Instead, it serves as a powerful assistant that can improve efficiency while still requiring human judgement and responsibility.
